# Drip Droplet Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 26, 2026

Drip Droplet ("the game") is made by Bob Bullock ("we", "us"). This policy explains how the game handles information. In short: **the game does not collect, store, share, or sell any personal information from anyone, including children.**

## Information we collect
**None.** The game does not ask for, collect, or transmit any personal information. That means no name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, voice, device identifiers (including the Android advertising ID), or usage or analytics data.

- There are **no accounts or sign-in**.
- There are **no advertisements** and **no in-app purchases**.
- There is **no chat, messaging, or sharing** with other players.
- The game does **not use analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising services**, and it does not send data to us or to any third party.

## Information stored on your device
The game saves your progress and settings (for example, levels completed and sound volume) **only on your device**. This information never leaves the device, and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

## Children's privacy
Drip Droplet is designed for players **ages 6 and up**, including children under 13, as well as teens and adults. Because the game collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children as defined by the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and no parental consent is needed to play. We follow the Google Play Families Policy. If you are a parent or guardian and have any questions or concerns, please contact us at the address below.

## Permissions
The game does not request access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or files. The game uses your device's vibration for gentle feedback during play. You can turn it off in Settings, and no data is involved.

## Third parties
The game is distributed through Google Play. When you download it, Google may process information under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive personal information about you from Google Play.

## Your rights
Because we hold no personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, or delete. Players, parents, and guardians in any region (including under the EU/UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws) may still contact us with questions.

## Changes to this policy
If a future version of the game changes how information is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date **before** that version is released. If such a change ever involved collecting information from children, we would obtain verifiable parental consent where the law requires it.
